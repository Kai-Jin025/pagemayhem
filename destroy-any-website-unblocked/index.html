Destroy Any Website Unblocked: How to Play When Your Network Says No

If you are searching for an "unblocked" version, here is the honest lay of the land — what works, what doesn't exist, and which shortcuts are actually traps.

First, the thing nobody tells you

"Unblocked" games usually mean one thing: a website that your school or workplace network has blocked, and a mirror site that hasn't been blocked yet. That model works for small embedded flash-style games. It does not describe Destroy Any Website, because Destroy Any Website is not hosted on a game portal — it lives on its developer's own website, alongside his other tools. There is no second copy to run to. If your network blocks that site, the game goes with it, and no legitimate "unblocked mirror" of it exists, because only its creator has the right to host it.

That leaves you with four real options, in increasing order of how much we can recommend them.

Option 1: Try the official games — one of them may already work

Networks almost never block "this genre" — they block specific domains, often after someone got caught. The genre's major games live on different domains: Destroy Any Website on its developer's site, Page Rage at page-rage.com, and Site Slayer as a browser add-on rather than a website at all. It is entirely possible that one loads beautifully while another is stuck behind the block. Our genre hub lists the full family — try the official homes, one at a time, and see what your network actually allows.

Option 2: Browser extensions sidestep the usual block entirely

The add-on route is the cleverest legitimate workaround in the genre. Site Slayer and the Page Rage extension don't require you to visit a game website: once installed, they operate on the page already in your browser. Half the fun of this genre is destroying your page anyway, so the add-on versions lose nothing. The catch is on your side of the fence: installing an extension needs your browser's add-on store to be reachable and unlocked. If both the game sites and the stores are blocked, this door closes too.

Option 3: The built-in pages, as a last resort

If a game does load but the URL-pasting step fails — or you just want zero friction — remember that these games ship with built-in destructible pages so nobody has to type an address at all. Destroy Any Website and Page Rage both include ready-made levels; Site Slayer's version is simply whatever tab you're on. When the network lets the game in but you want to stay subtle, the built-in pages are the quiet play.

Option 4: The options we do not recommend (and why)

  • Third-party "unblocked" mirror sites. Sites that rehost someone else's game on a fresh domain are the single sketchiest corner of this genre: malware, redirect chains, fake play buttons and phishing pages are routine. And for this game in particular, mirrors have no legitimate source to copy from — the developer has never released a rehostable build.
  • Proxy and VPN services on a school network. Whether these are acceptable is a question for your school's rules, not for us — but understand that many schools treat bypassing the filter as a far more serious violation than the game itself. Losing network access over a browser toy is a bad trade.
  • Anything that asks you to sign in "to play." No game in this genre has ever needed your account. That is a phishing page, full stop.

The boring but true advice

The games are free, they are on the open web, and they are not going anywhere in the next few days. If the network you are on right now blocks them, the winning move is to note it, move on, and enjoy the rampage at home on your own connection — where nobody minds, and where you can blast the page at full frame rate with the sound on. That's not us siding with your school. That's us pointing out that the alternative is malware with a play button.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official unblocked version of Destroy Any Website?

No — and you don't need one. Destroy Any Website is a normal website, not an app that can be unblocked. If your network blocks game sites in general, an official mirror would be blocked too. The realistic options are the ones listed on this page.

Is it safe to use third-party unblocked mirror sites?

We don't recommend it. Sites that rehost someone else's game under an unblocked domain are a magnet for malware, aggressive ads and phishing. The legitimate games in this genre are all free at their official homes — there is nothing a mirror can offer that the original does not.

Which game works best on restricted networks?

Browser add-ons like Site Slayer and the Page Rage extension work differently from game websites, since the destruction happens on the page you are already viewing. Whether you can install extensions depends on your network and device policy.

Should I use a proxy or VPN to get around a school block?

That depends entirely on your school's acceptable-use rules. Many schools treat proxy and VPN use as a separate, bigger violation than the game itself. On a network you don't own, follow the rules that apply to you — the games will still be there at home.

Why doesn't PageMayhem host an unblocked copy of the game?

Because the game belongs to its developer. We are a fan site: we write guides and comparisons, we don't rehost other people's games. Anything online claiming to offer a hosted copy of Destroy Any Website is doing something its creator never agreed to.
