Privacy Policy

Short version: PageMayhem is a static website. It has no accounts, no forms, and no interest in your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing personal. PageMayhem does not offer logins, newsletters or comment systems, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other identifying information. There is no tracking pixel of our own, and we do not sell, rent or trade data — because we don't have any to sell.

Like every website, our hosting provider processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to serve pages and keep the site online. That processing happens at the infrastructure level and is not used by us to identify visitors.

Analytics

If we ever add privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand which guides are useful, this page will be updated to say so before it happens — and it will remain aggregate: visit counts, not visitors.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to games and tools hosted by other people. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We choose links carefully and never link to sites that require an account or payment to play a free game, but we can't control what others do — read their policies as you would anywhere else.

Cookies and local storage

PageMayhem sets no advertising cookies, no tracking cookies and no social-media pixels. The site's own pages are served as static files and store nothing in your browser. Our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) may set strictly-necessary security cookies as part of serving any website; those exist to protect the site from abuse and are not used to build profiles of you.

If that ever changes — for example, if advertising or aggregate analytics are added later — this policy will be updated before the change goes live, and the change will be described here in plain language, not buried in a re-acceptance popup.

Your rights and questions

Because the site collects no personal data, there is no stored profile to access, correct or delete — a request to "show me everything you have on me" would return an empty page, proudly. If you believe something on the site nevertheless touches your privacy (for example, an image or link served from elsewhere that attempts to identify visitors), contact us and we will investigate and remove it if needed.

Children

This site is written for a general audience — the games it covers are free browser toys enjoyed by teenagers and adults alike. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included, so there is nothing to special-case; the answer is the same for every age group: no accounts, no data, nothing stored.

Changes to this policy

The current version of this policy always lives at this address and is the one that applies. Material changes will be described in this section rather than hidden, and we keep the language here readable on purpose: a privacy policy you can't understand is barely a policy at all.

Advertising, honestly

The site currently shows no ads at all. If ads are added later to cover hosting costs, they will be clearly labelled, will never be disguised as links or download buttons, and this policy will say plainly what kind of ad service is in use — including whether it uses cookies or personalised targeting — before it goes live.
